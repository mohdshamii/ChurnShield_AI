Methodological Rigor & Causality Notice
Ensuring scientific integrity and regulatory compliance.

1. Cooperative Game Theory: The Shapley value is the only attribution method satisfying Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy, and Additivity axioms. ChurnShield AI fits exact tree-based Shapley algorithms to eliminate heuristic approximations.
